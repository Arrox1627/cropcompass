cropcompass

Subject-aware cropping that runs in your browser. A small machine-learning model finds the people; edges and colour find everything else. Drop in a photo and see where a plain centre crop cuts someone off, and where cropcompass keeps them.

People and things are found by an EfficientDet-Lite2 model, run by Google's MediaPipe in your browser. The first photo downloads it once (about 17 MB), and it's cached after that.

or drop photos here. They never leave your browser.
Stage 1 finds the subjects: people and things with a small ML model, everything else from edges and colour on a 96 px copy. Stage 2 slides each frame's window onto them. How it works.
